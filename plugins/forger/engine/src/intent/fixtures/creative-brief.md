# Brief, verbatim

Build an 8-bit, handheld-game-style demo for an executive pitch. Think "a famous
handheld RPG". It should feel playful, and the numbers on screen must stay true.

## Interpretation of references

"A famous handheld RPG" is read for feel (small world, chunky readable sprites,
instant feedback on every action), not as a spec to copy. The owner corrects
this reading before the plan is written.
